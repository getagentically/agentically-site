How it works

Three steps from empty seat to working operator.

01

Hire your operators

Pick from the operator gallery or create your own — name, role, lane, and standing orders. Each one knows exactly what it owns and what it doesn't touch.

02

Talk to your HQ

Type or speak. Chief routes the work to the right operator. They research, draft, schedule, and build — on the web, on your desktop, or in the cloud while you sleep.

03

You approve, they ship

Nothing posts, sends, or spends without your explicit yes. Every deliverable lands in your approvals inbox as an exact final replica. You stay the boss.

FAQ

How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude?

Those are brilliant generalists in a chat tab. Agentically is the layer on top: named operators with defined roles, persistent memory of your business, standing orders, scheduled runs, and an approvals workflow — a system, not a conversation.

Can it really talk?

Yes. Voice works in the browser on every plan; HQ plans get premium natural voices. Talk to your operators from your phone or computer like a call with your team.

Will it do things without my permission?

No. The approval gate is the core of the product: nothing publishes, sends, spends, or ships until you approve the exact final version. Autonomy where it's safe, sign-off where it matters.

Phone or computer?

The web app works on both today. Desktop install is available with the one-time license; native mobile apps are on the roadmap.

What if I already use Claude?

Even better — Agentically is built to plug into Claude as a connector, so your operators are available right inside the tools you already use.
