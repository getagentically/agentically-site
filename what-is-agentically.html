What does "agentically" mean?

agenticallyADVERB

In the manner of an agent: acting with autonomy, initiative, and judgment toward a goal — rather than waiting to be told each step.

The word comes from agency — the capacity to act. When software works agentically, it doesn't just answer a question; it takes a goal, breaks it into steps, uses tools, and comes back with finished work. That shift — from chatbots that talk to agents that act — is the defining change in AI right now.
